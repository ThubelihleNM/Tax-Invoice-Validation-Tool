Frequently Asked Questions

What is tax invoice validation? +
Tax invoice validation is the automated process of scanning invoice documents, extracting relevant tax data, checking calculations, and ensuring government tax guidelines are met.
How does duplicate detection work? +
We record invoice metadata in a secured database. Newly scanned items are cross-referenced with historic entries within the preceding 90 days to prevent duplicate filings.
Can I upgrade to a Company account? +
Yes, you can edit your profile configuration and add company addresses, names, and tax numbers at any time from your dashboard account settings page.
